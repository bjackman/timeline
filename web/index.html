Linear axis — durations are to scale. Several ways to cross the 5 000 000 000 000× zoom range, to see which feels right: scroll to zoom (keep scrolling and it accelerates), pinch on a trackpad, right-drag up/down for continuous zoom, shift-drag to box-select a range, double-click to zoom in (shift to zoom out), +/−, arrows, 0 on the keyboard. The strip along the bottom is the navigator: all of time on a log axis, with your current window marked — click or drag it to travel, shift-drag to select. Drag to pan, click an event to open its article. Bars show date uncertainty — a geological event known only to the nearest million years is drawn a million years wide — except where an event has a real end date. Data from Wikidata (CC0).
